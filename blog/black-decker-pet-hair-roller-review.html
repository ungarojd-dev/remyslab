What worked

It actually picks up the hair. That sounds obvious but a lot of pet hair tools just push fur around — this one collects it into a compartment you empty out. No sticky tape to peel off and throw away, no replacement rolls to buy.

For around $16 it's a better long-term value than lint rollers. We've gone through enough of those to know the cost adds up.

What didn't work

Nothing major to flag. It's not magic — you still need to make a few passes on heavy fur areas. And like any roller it works best with long strokes in one direction rather than back and forth.

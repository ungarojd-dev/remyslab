What is Remy’s Lab?

Remy’s Lab is a dog product testing page connected to @remy.lab on TikTok. Remy, a yellow Labrador, tests dog products so dog owners can see current product notes, partner links, and discount codes in one simple place.

Some links may be affiliate links. If you use one, Remy’s Lab may earn a commission. That does not change which products get listed.

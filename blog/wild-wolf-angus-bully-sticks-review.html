What worked

Remy goes right for these with no convincing needed. He gets 1 to 1.5 hours out of a stick, which is solid for a 12-inch chew.

The sourcing is clean. Wild Wolf lists these as 100% natural Angus beef pizzle, lab-tested, hand-inspected, and packed in the USA. That is a straightforward ingredient story and the product backs it up.

The mixed thickness in the bag means some sticks are thicker than others. Remy got the thicker ones, which tracks with the longer chew time.

What did not work

Slightly more smell than the Mighty Paw sticks. Not a dealbreaker, but worth noting if odor is a priority for you. Still manageable.

As with any bully stick, supervise your dog and take the small end away when it gets short.

Leashes and walk gear

What is a climbing rope dog leash?

A climbing rope dog leash is a thicker rope style leash that usually feels sturdy in hand. For Remy, we care about grip comfort, clip security, weight, and whether it feels annoying on a normal walk. Related link: Pack Leashes code.

What leash length is best for a Lab?

For most normal walks, many dog owners like a leash that gives some sniff room without letting the dog get too far ahead. The right choice depends on your dog, where you walk, and how much control you need. Related test: Pack Leashes Dino Collar Set.

Training treats

What makes a good dog training treat?

A good training treat is small, easy to handle, motivating, and not so messy that you hate using it. With Remy, the main question is whether he cares enough to focus when distractions are around. Related partner: Pupford code.

Are freeze dried treats good for training?

Freeze dried treats can work well for training because they are usually lightweight and easy to break down. The tradeoff is that some can crumble in your pocket or treat pouch. We care most about motivation, mess, and portion size.

Chews

What is a bully stick?

A bully stick is a single ingredient style dog chew that many dogs get excited about. For Remy, we look at odor, chew time, mess, and whether the size makes sense for a Lab. Related test: Mighty Paw Bully Sticks.

How long should a bully stick last?

It depends on the dog, the thickness, and how hard they chew. A strong chewer can go through thinner sticks fast. We always watch Remy with chews and pay attention to size, smell, and how quickly he works through it. Related test: Wild Wolf Angus Bully Sticks.

Toys and enrichment

What is a dog puzzle toy?

A dog puzzle toy makes your dog work a little for food or treats. The goal is not just to keep them busy, it is to make them think without making the toy so hard that they give up. Related test: Potaroma Dog Puzzle Toy.

Grooming and cleanup

Are reusable pet hair rollers worth it?

Reusable pet hair rollers can be worth it if you are constantly using lint rollers on couches, beds, or clothes. The main test is whether it grabs hair without needing sticky sheets. Related test: BLACK+DECKER Pet Hair Roller.

Do rubber brooms work on carpet?

A rubber broom can pull pet hair out of carpet that a vacuum leaves behind. It can also be a workout, so it makes the most sense for rugs, thin carpet, or spot cleanup. Related test: FURemover Rubber Broom.

Dog accessories

Are dog bandanas safe?

Dog bandanas can be fine when they fit comfortably and are used with supervision. We care about fit, material feel, and whether Remy actually tolerates wearing it. Related partner: Brooks & Roo code.

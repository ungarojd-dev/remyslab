Leashes & Walk Gear FAQ

Simple answers for dog owners comparing everyday walk gear. When we have real Remy test notes, we link them instead of pretending every answer is a full review.

What is a climbing rope dog leash?

A climbing rope dog leash is a leash made with thick rope style material inspired by climbing rope. Dog owners usually look at this style because it feels sturdy, has a thicker hand feel, and can look more fun than a plain nylon leash. At Remy's Lab, we care most about grip comfort, clip security, leash weight, and whether it feels annoying after a normal walk with Remy.

What leash length is best for a Lab?

For normal neighborhood walks, many dog owners like a standard 5 to 6 foot leash because it gives the dog room to sniff without giving up too much control. For a young Lab, the best leash is usually the one you can hold comfortably while still keeping the dog close when cars, people, or other dogs are nearby.

What is a traffic handle on a dog leash?

A traffic handle is a second handle placed closer to the dog. It gives you a quick way to shorten the leash when crossing streets, passing another dog, or walking through a crowded area. It is not something every dog owner needs, but it can be useful if your dog gets excited or distracted on walks.

What is a carabiner leash clip?

A carabiner leash clip is a clip style closure used to attach the leash to a collar or harness. Dog owners usually care about whether it feels secure, whether it is easy to open with one hand, and whether it feels bulky. With Remy, clip security matters because he is young, strong, and very interested in whatever is happening across the street.

What makes a dog leash comfortable to hold?

A comfortable dog leash should feel good in your hand, not burn or pinch when the dog pulls, and not feel too heavy for the length of the walk. Texture matters. A leash can look great online, but the real test is whether you still like holding it after a normal walk with a strong dog.

What worked

It actually picks up the hair — not just pushes it around. The fur collects in a compartment you empty out. No sticky tape to peel, no replacement rolls to buy. For $16 it's better long-term value than lint rollers by a lot.

What didn't work

Nothing major. It's not instant — you need a few passes on heavy fur areas, and it works better with long strokes in one direction than back and forth.

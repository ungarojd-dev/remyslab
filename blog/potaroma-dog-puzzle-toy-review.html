What we tested

We tested the Potaroma 2-level puzzle toy by using it as a slow feeder and as a treat puzzle for Remy. The main question for this kind of product is simple: does it actually make a dog work for the food a little instead of just blasting through a normal bowl or easy treat drop?

For Remy, it gave him something to do and turned food time into more of an activity.

Slow-feeder use
The two-level setup helps turn food into more of an activity instead of a quick bowl dump.
Puzzle time
It works well as a food or treat puzzle when you want to keep Remy busy for a bit.
Everyday enrichment
This feels more like an enrichment toy than a throw-and-forget toy, which is how we would use it.

What worked

The biggest plus is the format. It is a 2-level puzzle toy, so it feels a little more engaging than a basic slow-feeder bowl. You can use it with food or treats, which makes it flexible depending on whether you are doing mealtime or just trying to keep your dog occupied for a few minutes.

It is also a nice enrichment option if you want your dog to work a little mentally instead of just eating as fast as possible.

What did not work

No major dealbreakers from our test. The main tradeoff is that this is more of a supervised enrichment toy than a toy you toss on the floor and forget about. Like most food puzzles, you also have to take the extra step to load it up and clean it after use.

Remy's take

Remy did what Remy usually does with food involved and stayed interested. It gave him something to work on, which is really the whole point of a puzzle toy like this.

Worth buying?

Yes, if you want a simple puzzle toy that can double as a slow feeder and a treat dispenser. It is a nice option for adding a little enrichment to your dog's routine without overcomplicating it.

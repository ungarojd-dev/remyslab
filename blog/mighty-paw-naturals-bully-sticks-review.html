What worked

The biggest win is how simple they are. The listing identifies a single ingredient: beef pizzle. Mighty Paw also describes the sticks as grass-fed beef chews with no added flavors or artificial preservatives.

They are also noticeably lower odor than other bully sticks we have tried. Lower odor does not mean zero odor, but the difference is real enough that we keep reaching for these instead of the other bags.

What did not work

Nothing major in our testing. They are still bully sticks, so there is some smell and they are not a quick little training treat. As with any chew, we supervise Remy and take away the small end when it is time.
